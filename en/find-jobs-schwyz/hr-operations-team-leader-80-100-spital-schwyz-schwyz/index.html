Full description

Welcome to the hospital Schwyz! Health is our highest good. As a modern acute care hospital, we offer expertise and state-of-the-art infrastructure at a high level in basic and special care. We work hand in hand across all areas in order to optimally care for our patients and improve their current situation with the treatments tailored to them. Their well-being is our top priority. We stand for this with uncompromising quality and humanity. Do you like to lead a team, think in processes and enjoy actively developing operational personnel work? Do you see HR as a service provider and sparring partner for managers and employees and would like to shape efficient, future-oriented HR processes? To strengthen our HR team, we are looking for a team leader immediately or by agreement:n Team Leader:in HR Operations (80-100%) In this versatile function, you will take over the personnel and professional leadership of the operational HR team. They ensure a professional and efficient handling of HR processes, continuously develop them and ensure a high quality of service to managers and employees. Your challenge

  • Professional and personnel management of the operational HR team, promotion of employees and ensuring an efficient distribution of tasks and cooperation.
  • Overall responsibility for the operational HR processes along the Employee Lifecycle – from entry to exit. Ensure uniform standards, clear processes and high process quality.
  • Analysis and continuous development of existing HR processes. Identification of optimization potentials as well as initiation and implementation of improvements and digitalization projects.
  • Ensuring competent and service-oriented support for managers and employees in all operational HR issues.
  • Active participation in day-to-day business including responsibility for a support area.
  • Management and participation in HR projects as well as close cooperation with internal interfaces and external partners. You are a leadership-strong, initiative personality with a pronounced understanding of processes and enjoyment of the further development of a modern HR organization. They combine a structured and solution-oriented working method with a high service orientation. They have the following qualifications:
  • Basic commercial training with further training in HR (e.g. HR specialist or equivalent),
  • Several years of professional experience in operational HR, ideally with first management experience
  • Sound knowledge of Swiss labour and social security law
  • Experience in the optimization and further development of HR processes
  • Strong process understanding and affinity for digitalization and HR systems
  • Leadership competence, communication strength and pleasure in the development of employees
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • High service orientation, initiative and team spirit
  • Experience in the healthcare sector is welcome, but no requirement Our offer expects you to have a responsible leadership role in which you can actively shape and further develop operational HR work.
  • Versatile area of responsibility with scope for design and responsibility for the further development of HR processes
  • Motivated, collaborative team and close cooperation with different areas of the hospital
  • An organization with clear structures and short decision paths
  • Family, interprofessional working environment
  • Attractive employment conditions and a place of work with a lot of quality of life Interested?
