17.09.2026 Schwyz Goldau

Become part of our team!

Raiffeisenbank Rigi with its locations Küssnacht, Goldau, Schwyz, Brunnen and Illgau is a successful and regionally oriented bank with lived values (focused, committed, authentic and collaborative) and a clear view of the future. For the offices Schwyz and Goldau we are looking for an independent, committed and service-oriented personality as a private customer consultant: in 80-100%.

What awaits you?

  • Competent and holistic advice to customers: in all financial matters, especially in the areas of figures/savings, investing, financing and pensions
  • Expansion of the existing customer base through targeted acquisition of new customers and further development of existing customers
  • Cooperation in target group-specific market development measures, in particular contact campaigns and performance of representative tasks
  • Close cooperation with our specialists in the various specialist areas

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking training
  • Further training in business administration and/or banking
  • Years of experience in the financial services sector
  • In-depth knowledge of finance, investment and pension schemes
  • Customer and sales-oriented personality with acquisition skills
  • Communication-strong, flexible and resilient person

You manage to inspire customers with your consulting skills, commitment and understanding? Then you are right with us! Get to know our dynamic RB Rigi culture and celebrate success together with us. I look forward to your application. Marc Hunold Team Leader Private Client Services Why Raiffeisen?
