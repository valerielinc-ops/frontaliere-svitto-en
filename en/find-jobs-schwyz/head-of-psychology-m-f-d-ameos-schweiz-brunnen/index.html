Full description

To strengthen our team at the AMEOS Seeklinikum Brunnen, we are looking for an initiative and committed personality as of November 2026 or by agreement.

Head of Psychology (m/f/d) — 80-100%, stationary

Their advantages We offer you the opportunity to become part of our family team. You can expect a versatile, attractive working environment in which you take care of patients on one of our stations. Furthermore, you can expect a structured training and supervision program. Our clinic is recognised by the SIWF of the FMH as a B-Spital, which means that eligible time for the Swiss Federal Recognition and the arrangement model can be acquired. Our work location Brunnen is located in an environment with a high quality of life and many leisure activities and is very easy to reach from Lucerne, Zug or Zurich by all means of transport, including by ship.

Their tasks

  • Operational, operational, technical and human resources management of the functional unit
  • Collaboration in the management of the overall clinic, for example divisional management
  • Budget responsibility and management within the framework of the existing rules of competence
  • Management of employees of various professional disciplines in the area of responsibility
  • Ensuring the professional training of assistant psychologists, assistant doctors and psychology interns
  • Ensuring and coordinating the interdisciplinary care of patients in the area of responsibility
  • Consideration and guarantee of compliance with legal bases
  • Cooperation in strategic and conceptual tasks
  • Promoting interprofessional cooperation
  • Ensuring an optimal flow of information within the area of responsibility as well as to supervisors and other relevant bodies
  • Active participation in meetings and team meetings as well as contributing experience from further education and training
  • Competences/responsibilities
  • Situational management of employees in the area of responsibility
  • Conscientious performance of the delegated tasks and safeguarding of tasks in their own absence
  • Maintenance of professional exchange in the interdisciplinary treatment team

Your profile

  • They have a Swiss Federal Recognition in Psychotherapy and ideally several years of experience in this profession.
  • Experience in team leadership is an advantage.
  • They are open to interdisciplinary work in a team and reliable in documentation.
  • Previous experience in sleep medicine is welcome, in-depth familiarization desired.
  • They have strong leadership and communication skills and actively promote professional and interprofessional exchange.
