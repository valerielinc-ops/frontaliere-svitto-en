YOUR MISSION

As part of a 12-month HOM, you ensure the polishing, polishing and finishing of complete components in production and provide customer service, in accordance with the quality criteria of the brand.

HOW ARE YOU GOING TO HAVE A IMPACT?

Carry out the various preparation and finishing operations: grinding or stone grinding/felting, softening, emeralding/vivage, satining, sanding, microboiling, cabling...

  • Mount and adjust tools and abrasives necessary for different polishing operations.
  • Ensure the positioning and maintenance of the parts taking into account their characteristics and level of finish.
  • Perform polishing operations in accordance with the operating ranges and quality requirements of the House.
  • Perform the necessary alterations on finished components and products as part of the Customer Service interventions.
  • Perform dimensional and aesthetic checks of the parts and ensure their conformity.
  • Apply rigorously the quality monitoring plan.
  • Identify and report any anomalies and contribute to finding solutions and continuous improvement.
  • Ensure compliance with the quality, timeliness and pace requirements of the Customer Service.
  • Respect the hygiene, safety and safety rules applicable at the workplace and in the Manufacture.

HOW ARE YOU TO BE US?

You have a polisher CAP/CFC, or have equivalent training in the industrial environment.

You ideally justify 5 to 8 years of polishing experience, acquired in industrial workshop and, ideally, in a Customer Service / VAS environment.

You master several polishing techniques and have excellent aesthetic sensitivity. You can read a technical plan and are recognized for your dexterity, accuracy and thoroughness.

Patient, rigorous and careful, you are particularly attentive to the quality of the work done. Your flexibility, agility and team spirit will allow you to fully integrate into our Customer Service.
