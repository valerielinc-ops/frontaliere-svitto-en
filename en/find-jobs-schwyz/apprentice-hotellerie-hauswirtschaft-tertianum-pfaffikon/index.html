Full description

The Tertianum Residence Huob, in the center of Pfäffikon SZ, consists of 67 modern apartments with in-house Spitex service and a rehabilitation, care and dementia living area with 21 single rooms, internal restaurant and public restaurant ROSSOROSSO, physiotherapy, fitness, therapy bath, coiffeur salon, podology and cosmetics studio.

For the Tertianum Residence Huob we are looking for you at 1. August 2027 for the

Apprenticeship as a specialist hotel industry EFZ or practitioner hotel industry EBA

You will learn this with us

  • General cleaning work
  • Laundry supply and ordering
  • Care for guests and take over the service
  • Implementation of operational processes and compliance with work quality requirements
  • Decorating rooms and premises

You bring that with you

  • Completed school education (secondary school A or B) with good academic achievements
  • Empathy and patience as well as interest in dealing with our guests
  • Careful appearance, good manners as well as hygiene awareness and sense of order
  • Creativity and an eye for detail
  • A warm and humorous basic attitude
  • Enjoying versatile challenges and practical work
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Schwyz?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Schwyz's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Schwyz's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
