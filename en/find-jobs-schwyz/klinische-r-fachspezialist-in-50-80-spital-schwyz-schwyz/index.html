Your challenge

  • Caring for patients in musculoskeletal, oncological and increasingly geriatric rehabilitation
  • Taking on delegated medical tasks within your area of competence in close collaboration with the medical management
  • Carrying out rounds, clinical examinations and discussions with patients and relatives
  • Preparing medical prescriptions in the areas of medication, diagnostics and therapy
  • Writing progress, discharge and other medical reports
  • Coordinating and supporting the interdisciplinary treatment process
  • Close collaboration with the Medical team, nursing and therapeutic services to ensure high-quality patient care
  • Contact person for nurses in the rehabilitation ward as well as employees in the therapeutic departments
  • Participation in medical and interdisciplinary reports, case discussions and quality development processes Your profile:
  • Training as a HF/FH nursing specialist with several years of professional experience, preferably in an acute hospital
  • Completed further training CAS Clinical specialist or the willingness to complete this further training
  • Strong interest in clinical issues as well as in interprofessional collaboration
  • Innovative, committed and solution-oriented personality with a high level of initiative
  • High social competence and excellent communication skills
  • Responsible, resilient and flexible way of working
  • Organizational skills and structured way of working
  • Very good knowledge of spoken and written German Our offer:
  • A responsible and varied job with expanded clinical skills and a high degree of personal responsibility
  • Attractive development opportunity for experienced people Nursing professionals with an interest in an expanded clinical role
  • Active participation in the further development of our rehabilitation offerings and clinical processes
  • Close interprofessional collaboration with the rehabilitation doctors
  • Exciting, future-oriented working environment in an evolving rehabilitation department
  • Appreciative collaboration in a committed and collegial team
  • Generous support for your professional development through training and further education opportunities
  • Very good accessibility by public transport (incl.
