About the Spitex Training Association Innerschwyz The Spitex Training Association Innerschwyz is a cooperative association of five Spitex organizations for training in nursing. Apprenticeship as a specialist health EFZ (FaGe) for 2027 You want an apprenticeship that makes sense and has a future! You really want to support people where their lives take place: at home ? Then the FaGe apprenticeship in the training association Innerschwyz could be exactly your thing. With us you get to know care in many ways, namely in the middle of everyday life: you are on the road, see different life situations and quickly realize how much you can achieve with small actions. And yes: It is sometimes demanding. But that's what gets you moving forward. What to expect In your 3-year apprenticeship, you learn step by step everything you need as a FaGe – accompanied by experienced trainers and teams who take you and your needs seriously.

With us you will learn

  • Supporting people in everyday life (e.g. personal care, dressing, eating, mobilization)
  • to observe, think and take responsibility
  • safely carry out simple medical-technical activities (always suitable for the level of training)
  • Communicating professionally – with clients, relatives and in a team
  • to document and work according to clear quality standards

What is special about the training network

You benefit from a strong network, the choice of three training locations, joint high quality training and an education that makes sense and has a future. If you recognize yourself here, it fits pretty well

  • You like working with people – young or old.
  • You are curious and want to learn something (not just “get through”).
  • You take responsibility and trust yourself to become more independent.
  • You are open, friendly and reliable.
  • You have completed compulsory schooling (or soon) and are ready for the next step.

Every year, the Spitex Regio Arth-Goldau is involved in the training of health professionals EFZ and qualified nurses HF within the framework of the training association Innerschwyz. Five Spitex organizations pursue a common goal: to awaken enthusiasm for outpatient care and to ensure a modern, high-quality education. As an apprentice, you get to know a wide range of working methods, quickly build up a valuable network and develop your skills in an open and meaningful environment. Through the intensive exchange between the organizations you also acquire valuable skills for your later work in the care.
