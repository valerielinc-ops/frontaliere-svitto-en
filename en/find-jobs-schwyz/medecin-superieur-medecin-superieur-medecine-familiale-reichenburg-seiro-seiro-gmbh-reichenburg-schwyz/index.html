Full description

In Reichenburg in the canton of Schwyz, you combine family medical care with medical and human leadership responsibility. They lead the practice team of doctors, doctors and medical practice assistants, are responsible for the medical processes and form the interface to the commercial management. For diagnosis and treatment, you have your own laboratory, X-ray, ultrasound, ECG, spirometry and a practice pharmacy at your disposal. The position is to be filled by agreement in a capacity of 70 to 100 %. What awaits you in this task

  • Family medical work with history, diagnosis, therapy and prevention
  • Professional and personnel management of the practice team including recruitment and employee interviews
  • Responsibility for medical practice processes as well as for pharmacy, laboratory, X-ray and other infrastructure
  • Interface function between group practice and commercial management
  • Performing small-surgical procedures and treating chronic wounds
  • Participation in internal and external quality circles and in the regional medical emergency service

This is what they bring with them.

  • Specialist General Internal Medicine / Specialist General Internal Medicine or an equivalent specialist title recognized by MEBEKO
  • Several years of professional experience in general internal medicine or family medicine
  • Leadership experience or the clear willingness to take responsibility for a practice team
  • Experience in independent medical work and in long-term patient care
  • Very good knowledge of German at least level C1

That sets them apart.

  • They combine medical freedom of choice with responsibility for a functioning practice
  • You guide employees clearly, bindingly and comprehensibly
  • They make medical decisions independently and based on evidence
  • You can safely prioritize clinical, personnel and organizational issues
  • You work reliably with doctors, medical assistants and the management

This is what your future employer offers you

  • Guaranteed fixed salary plus additional revenue share
  • Permanent employment of 70 to 100%
  • At least 5 weeks of vacation
  • Regulated working hours
  • Leadership function with medical design scope
  • Administrative discharge by an experienced practice team
  • Own laboratory, X-ray, ultrasound, ECG, spirometry and practice pharmacy
  • Dermatological consultation by a specialist in the same building
  • Costs of continuing education and training
  • Hospitation on site to get to know each other before a definitive commitment
  • Professional exchange within a medical practice network
  • Privately managed family business without corporate, pharmaceutical or insurance influence on medical decisions

Additionally for applicants from the EU
