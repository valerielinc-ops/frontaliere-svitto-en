Full description

Welcome to the hospital Schwyz. Health is our highest good. That is why we do everything every day to help people – professionally and personally. As a regionally anchored hospital, we care about the direct relationship with the population of the region and their trust. The extensive range of medical care and modern infrastructure make the Schwyz Hospital attractive for people from all over Central Switzerland – and beyond. Do you enjoy managing complex projects and actively shaping strategic projects? Then you shape the future of our hospital with us as: Project Manager: in corporate development 80 - 100% In this versatile function, you support the hospital management in the management of the hospital-wide project portfolio, independently lead strategic projects and accompany organizational changes. You work closely with the management and the various departments. Your challenge

  • Management of strategic projects: Independent planning and management of strategic projects, in particular for the further development of services and infrastructure projects. You are responsible for project goals, milestones, risks and successful implementation.
  • Project Management & Change Management: Coordination of interdisciplinary project teams and stakeholders, monitoring of deadlines and project goals as well as supporting organizational change processes.
  • Project Portfolio & Methodology: Participation in the management of the hospital-wide project portfolio, further development of project management standards and implementation of project reviews and lessons learned workshops.
  • Process Analysis & Requirements: Analysis of processes, collection of requirements and identification of optimization potential in close coordination with the relevant specialist areas. Your profile
  • Master's degree in economics or social sciences, health management, organisational development or comparable
  • Experience in independent management of projects, ideally in healthcare
  • In-depth knowledge of project management methods such as HERMES or PRINCE2 and/or agile approaches
  • Experience in accompanying change processes and organizational development
  • Structured, independent working method with high quality standards and flair for details
  • Strong communication and moderation skills as well as diplomatic skills and humour
  • Knowledge of the Swiss healthcare system is an advantage
  • very good oral and written knowledge of German Our offer
  • A versatile function with responsibility for strategically relevant projects and great scope for design
  • Close cooperation with the hospital management, the management and the departments in an interdisciplinary environment
  • The opportunity to actively shape the further development of the hospital and organizational change processes
  • An attractive place to work in a region with a high quality of life
  • Good social benefits and a super-compulsory pension fund solution Interested?
