Full description

The Role

  • Set our security priorities and see fixes through until they are verified.
  • Secure our cloud and our identities on Google Cloud and Kubernetes: access rights, keys and secrets, network exposure and backups.
  • Build logging and detection, and automate checks so they run without you.
  • Work with the engineers on application security: threat modelling, review of sensitive code paths, checks in the build pipeline and patch management.
  • Design the controls around payouts and the separation between partners.
  • Lead incident response, including the plan and the exercises.
  • Run our security testing: a yearly external penetration test and automated, AI-assisted testing in between.
  • Write the few rules we need, for customer data and for partners, vendors and AI agents, short enough that people follow them.

What you bring

  • 6+ years in engineering, at least 3 of them hands-on in security. You started in backend, platform or infrastructure work.
  • Strong cloud and identity security, ideally on Google Cloud, and experience with Kubernetes.
  • Solid application security: you can review code and show an engineer the fix.
  • You have built security in a small or growing company, not only worked inside a large security team.
  • You have led the response to a security incident.
  • You use AI tools every day.
  • Fluent English.

Nice to have

  • PHP, or TypeScript and Node.
  • Database security on MySQL or PostgreSQL.
  • Multi-tenant platforms and payment or payout flows.
  • Single sign-on and build pipeline security.
  • Swiss and EU data protection law.
  • German.

Why join us?

  • The whole problem. You build the security function and lead it as it grows, with the backing of the management.
  • Hands on the systems. This is an engineering role. There is no team to manage at the start, and policies and audits are a small part of the work.
  • Real stakes. Royalties and customer data for artists and labels run through the systems you protect.
  • AI-native team. Agents are tools you use and actors you secure.
  • Flexibility. Remote with flexible hours, and an office in Zurich when you want it.

Our stack Google Cloud and Kubernetes, GitLab, TypeScript and Node, PHP, MySQL and PostgreSQL.

How to apply

Apply with your CV and a few lines on a security control you built: what it was, what your part was, and whether it still runs. Please apply only through the application form linked to this ad. We cannot consider applications sent by email. What happens next: a short first call, a practical exercise, and a conversation with the engineers you would work with.

About us

iGroove is a profitable Swiss music company. We deliver music to the streaming services, pay out royalties to artists and labels, and build Label OS, the platform independent labels run their business on. Partners also run the platform under their own brand.

We are a small, senior team, and we work with AI agents every day, in every division.
