24.11.2026 Pfäffikon SZ

Become part of our team!

Whether payment transactions, customer administration or digital banking processes: They ensure that everything runs smoothly and thus make an important contribution to the customer experience at our bank. As Employee Services (m/f/d), you take on a versatile back office function and contribute to the efficient and smooth functioning of our internal processes. Exciting tasks, a collegial environment and the opportunity to take on responsibility await you. Have you become curious? Then we look forward to meeting you and introducing you to this exciting position at the Raiffeisenbank region on the left of Lake Zurich.

What do you expect?

  • Ensuring smooth payment transactions
  • Versatile processing and maintenance of our customer master data
  • Processing of bank accounts and means of payment
  • Direct answers to customer inquiries via our e-banking channel
  • Support for Bancomaten management
  • Independent conduct of investigations and varied administrative activities

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking studies or comparable commercial training
  • First banking experience, ideally in the back office
  • Networked thinking and a good understanding of internal banking processes
  • Team spirit and an open, service-oriented personality
  • Digital competence and enjoyment of efficient, system-supported processes

Our team ensures every day that everything works smoothly in the background. What counts here is team spirit, trust and the willingness to work together. We are also looking forward to this with new colleagues. Ulrich Roentgen Team Leader Services (ad interim) Why Raiffeisen?

We promote personal and professional development

Lifelong learning is part of our culture. We create space for individual professional and personal development and support our employees in a targeted manner – with a wide range of internal training opportunities and a cost share for external further education.

We maintain a family environment

Cooperative values are our fundamental pillars: togetherness. For each other. They shape our cooperation and our interaction with each other. The Du culture creates personal closeness and eye level across all functional levels.

We enable the purchase of additional holiday weeks

Our employees can purchase up to two additional holiday weeks per year in addition to the regular holiday days.

We offer attractive conditions and social benefits

Our employees benefit from exclusive advantages in banking products, various purchase discounts at third parties and an above-average retirement provision.

We offer a collective participation in success instead of individual bonuses

We bring collective performance to the fore. That is why we have a collective share of success instead of individual bonus payments. This promotes a healthy corporate culture in which overarching goals are pursued together.

The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Schwyz's migration office after the contract is signed.
