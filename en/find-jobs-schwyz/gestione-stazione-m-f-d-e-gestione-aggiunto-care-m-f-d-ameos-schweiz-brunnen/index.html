Full description

The AMEOS Seeklinikum Brunnen specializes in inpatient setting on stress secondary diseases (depressions, burnout, anxiety diseases, non-organic insomnias) and has a high level of competence in this area. To strengthen our team, we are looking for 01. June 2027 or by agreement an initiative and committed personality as

Station management (m/f/d) and Deputy management Care (m/f/d) — 100%

Their advantages

  • An interesting and demanding job in a well-functioning team
  • Close cooperation in an interdisciplinary team of nursing, psychology and medicine
  • Freedom of design and active participation in the treatment process
  • Generous internal and external training opportunities
  • An appreciative, respectful and collegial working environment
  • Attractive and contemporary employment conditions
  • A place of work with a high quality of life and good accessibility from Lucerne, Zug and Zurich
  • Exclusive day work – no night work

Their tasks

  • about 30 - 40% management tasks and 60 - 70% work as a qualified nurse HF
  • You take over the technical and personnel management of the station
  • They ensure a professional care process and a high quality of care on the station
  • You are responsible for scheduling the daytime service of all three care stations
  • You support the Head of Care in personnel, organizational and administrative tasks
  • During the absence of the manager take care of They are their representatives.
  • They carry out direct reference work on a day-to-day basis
  • You lead nursing therapy groups and take over the co-management of well-being groups
  • They promote constructive interprofessional cooperation and shape a working environment that supports the professional and personal development of employees.

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a qualified nurse HF as well as professional experience in the psychiatric environment
  • You have several years of leadership experience and have completed further training in management or leadership or are currently in a corresponding further education
  • You are an independent, initiative, communicative and structured personality and like to take on leadership responsibility
  • They are characterized by a high resilience, flexibility and willingness to take responsibility
  • You have strong social skills and enjoy working interdisciplinary
  • Your professional and personal development is an important concern for you
  • They convince with very good organizational skills and networked thinking
  • You have good PC application knowledge
  • You master the German language at native level or at least at level C2 and have several years of professional experience in a German-speaking environment
