Border zones for this permit have been abolished; the fiscal 20 km condition is separate. https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/it/home/themen/aufenthalt/eu_efta/ausweis_g_eu_efta.html Article 9 preserves exclusive Swiss taxation of the covered salary for old cross-border workers, with no automatic expiry in 2033 or 2034. New fiscal cross-border workers pay Swiss withholding at 80% of the ordinary rate and Italian tax with a credit for Swiss tax.
