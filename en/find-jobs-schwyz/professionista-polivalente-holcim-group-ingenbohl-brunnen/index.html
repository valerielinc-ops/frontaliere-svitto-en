Full description

Do you feel as comfortable on large machines as you do on varied tasks? Then you are exactly right with us. In this versatile function, you make sure that everything runs smoothly in our gravel plant – from production to machine operation to maintenance work.

What to expect

  • You carry out internal trips with the truck.
  • You serve the wheel loader and take care of the watering of the factory roads.
  • You support in the production and processing of grit and gravel.
  • You operate and maintain mobile crushing and screening systems.
  • You will perform minor maintenance and maintenance work on machines and equipment.
  • You take over cleaning and maintenance work on plants, buildings, in the workshop and on the company premises.
  • You support holiday representatives and take on other versatile operational tasks.
  • You handle machines and plants safely and environmentally consciously and consistently comply with our safety standards.

What you bring

  • You have completed technical vocational training, ideally supplemented by experience in handling construction machinery. An embodiment as a construction machine operator is advantageous.
  • You have a category C driving licence.
  • You bring technical understanding and interest in production, machinery and modern technologies.
  • You work independently, reliably and in a structured manner and have a strong sense of security.
  • You are team-oriented, communication-strong and service-oriented.
  • You show initiative, are willing to learn and enjoy taking on a wide range of tasks.

Our offer

  • Onboarding & getting started: Look forward to a team-oriented, open and future-oriented working environment. We accompany you from the beginning with a careful training, practical and structured, so that you can start successfully.
  • Career & Development: We create opportunities. With us, you are at the center as a person with your individual strengths and potential. Anyone who wants to is supported by us and can achieve a lot.
  • Appreciation & Team Culture: With us, you are part of a strong and inclusive team that holds together, shares ideas and masters challenges together. A trusting leadership culture ensures that your commitment is seen and appreciated.
  • Flexibility & Work-Life-Balance: We offer flexible working hours, 41 hours, part-time opportunities, 25-30 days holidays, holiday purchase, extended maternity and paternity leave
  • Attractive compensation: We offer our employees an attractive compensation package with many additional benefits and a very good pension fund
  • Employee benefits: From Reka checks to shares at a preferential price to the Swibeco advantage world - we make sure that you feel comfortable and benefit from attractive additional services
