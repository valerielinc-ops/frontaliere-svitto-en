Become part of our team!

Raiffeisenbank Rigi with its locations Küssnacht, Goldau, Schwyz, Brunnen and Illgau is a successful and regionally oriented bank with lived values (focused, committed, authentic and collaborative) and a clear view of the future. For our offices we are looking for an independent, committed and service-oriented personality as a financial planner: in 80-100%.

What do you expect?

  • Implementation of comprehensive financial and pension planning
  • Development of needs-based solutions that take into account the individual needs, goals, wishes and life situations of the customers
  • Assist and support our consultants in the active implementation of the defined measures
  • Identifying potential and cross-selling opportunities from financial planning
  • Participate in customer events and support our sales staff with expertise

What do you bring?

  • Completed banking training
  • Financial advisor or financial planner training or equivalent
  • Years of experience in the financial services sector
  • Customer and sales-oriented personality with acquisition skills
  • Independent and goal-oriented working method as well as pleasure in customer contact
  • Motivated and Dynamic Personality
  • Communication-strong, flexible and resilient person
