Full description

Welcome to the hospital Schwyz. Health is our highest good. That’s why we do everything every day to help people – professionally and personally. As a regionally anchored hospital, we care about the direct relationship with the population of the region and their trust. The extensive range of medical care and modern infrastructure make the Schwyz Hospital attractive for people from all over Central Switzerland – and beyond. Our pain center, attached to anesthesia, extends its offer to the department of rheumatology. To support our newly entering rheumatologist, we are looking for a committed and independent medical practice assistant as of 01.01.2027: in 70% Do you like to keep an overview, think along and ensure in the background that everything runs smoothly for our patients? For the secretariat anesthesia and the pain center, we are looking for a personality who can help shape our everyday practice with organizational talent, empathy and expertise. Your challenge

  • Organization and preparation of consultation hours and therapies for doctors: inside and Pain Nurses
  • Reception and care of our patients: inside
  • Working with our electronic systems (CGM, Polypoint RAP, PEP, OPALE, lbiCare)
  • Power measurement and digital document storage
  • Preparation of medical reports and general secretarial work
  • Telephone information and coordination
  • Possibility for assistance in punctures and infiltrations in rheumatology
  • Possibility to extend clinical activities, e.g. in rheumatology
  • Mutual Holiday Representation in the Secretariat Anesthesia Your Profile
  • Completed training as a medical practice assistant: in or FaGe with IT affinity
  • Good knowledge of medical terminology
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
  • Good PC user knowledge
  • Organised, independent and structured working methods
  • Motivated and thought-provoking personality characterized by their teamwork Our offer
  • Varied work in a small, collegial and interdisciplinary team
  • Design leeway and possibility to co-develop the new subject area
  • Workplace with public transport (mobility financing) and car park
  • Entry into a growing, modern company with future prospects
  • Modern infrastructure, professional equipment and materials
  • Supercompulsory pension fund solution, we take over 60% of the contributions
  • Opportunity for in-depth professional training with diploma within the framework of the Rheumaliga KOMPASS programme. Interested?
