Full description

Would you like to sit at the front left and drive our postal buses? Then let us train you as a PostBus driver:in and accompany us on our success story. Because with you we not only bring people to their destination, but also ensure first-class service on the road.

You can do that.

  • In the first four months, you will receive theoretical and practical driving lessons in order to optimally prepare for the category D exams. After successfully completing the exam, we introduce you as a driver:in on our lines.
  • After that you are ready and we offer you a permanent job.
  • In everyday life you bring our passengers safely and punctually from Monday to Sunday on our varied regional lines to their destination.
  • You are the host: in our post buses and advise our customers on tariff and timetable issues.
  • As a driver: you move much more than just your vehicle - on the road and in direct customer contact, you create positive experiences for our customers every day.

You bring that with you

  • You have basic training and are ready to obtain the category D driving licence - we will cover the costs of the training!
  • To obtain the category D driving licence, bring the category B (minimum 2 years) or C (minimum 3 months) driving licence.
  • Reliability is one of your strengths, and you appreciate the variety that irregular working hours (including Sundays and public holidays) bring.
  • You have very good knowledge of German and are well versed in using digital devices.
