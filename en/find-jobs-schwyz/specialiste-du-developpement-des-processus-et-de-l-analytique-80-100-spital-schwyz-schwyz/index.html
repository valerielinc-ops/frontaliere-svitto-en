Full description

Welcome to the hospital Schwyz. Health is our highest good. That is why we do everything every day to help people – professionally and personally. As a regionally anchored hospital, we care about the direct relationship with the population of the region and their trust. The extensive range of medical care and modern infrastructure make the Schwyz Hospital attractive for people from all over Central Switzerland – and beyond. You enjoy translating data into concrete insights, further developing processes and advancing digital solutions? Then you shape the future of our hospital with us as: Specialist: in Process Development & Analytics 80 - 100% In this versatile function, you work closely with the management, IT, controlling and other departments and actively shape the digital development of our hospital. Your challenge

  • Analytics & Reporting: Development of evaluations and key figures for management and departments, analysis and preparation of data from relevant source systems, further development of management reporting
  • Digitalization & Projects: Identification and implementation of digitalization, automation and AI potentials, management and support of digital projects, coordination with IT, controlling and specialist areas
  • Processes & Requirements: Analysis and optimization of business processes, collection and structuring of requirements, support for system evaluations and the introduction of digital solutions
  • Quality & Data Protection: Support in process and quality management as well as in certifications, cooperation in data protection topics and data protection impact assessments Your profile
  • University degree in business administration, business informatics, data science/statistics, process management, digitization or comparable
  • Experience in data preparation and analysis, reporting and process optimisation
  • Experience in managing/collaborating with projects
  • Interest in digitalization, new technologies and AI
  • Analytical, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Strength of communication and pleasure in working with different specialist areas
  • Self-employment, initiative and responsibility
  • very good oral and written knowledge of German Our offer
  • A very versatile role with great scope for design and the possibilities to further develop strategically relevant topics
  • Close cooperation with different disciplines in a collegial, interdisciplinary environment
  • An attractive place to work in a region with a high quality of life
  • Trusted working time and scope in shaping your everyday work.
  • Good social benefits and a super-compulsory pension fund solution Interested?
