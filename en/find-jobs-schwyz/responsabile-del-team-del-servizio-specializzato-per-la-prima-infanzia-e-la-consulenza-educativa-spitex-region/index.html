Full description

Do you want to strengthen parents in the first years of the family and lead a small specialist team? With us you connect the management of the specialist centre Early Childhood with the advice of parents and caregivers of children up to five years. Team management specialist centre Early childhood and parental counselling Their tasks

  • They lead the specialist office and the consultants professionally and personnel and coordinate team development and quality assurance.
  • They advise families on education, development and cohabitation in everyday life. If necessary, provide further support.
  • You cultivate the interdisciplinary exchange and develop the consulting offer with your team.

This is what they bring with them.

  • A completed education at the tertiary level with further education consultant Early childhood with federal diploma and / or parental counselling.
  • A leadership education or initial leadership experience and a willingness to take on the leadership of a small team.
  • Empathy, clear communication and an independent, well-organized way of working.

This is what awaits you with us A versatile task with direct contact with families, a well-established team and space for your ideas. We offer contemporary employment conditions and support your professional development.
