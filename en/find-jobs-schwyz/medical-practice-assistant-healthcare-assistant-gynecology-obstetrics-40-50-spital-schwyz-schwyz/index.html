Full description

Your challenge

  • Competent care of patients, referring physicians and doctors - in person, by telephone and in writing
  • Supporting doctors during consultation hours as well as planning and coordinating appointments using an electronic agenda
  • Carrying out diagnostic and nursing activities such as blood samples (venous and capillary), glucose tolerance tests, vaccinations, injections, CTG checks and vital parameter surveys
  • Creating and monitoring infusions
  • Organizing and coordinating direct operations in collaboration with referring doctors
  • Interface function between the medical team and bed scheduling
  • Responsibility for daily mail processing as well as the administration, archiving and provision of patient files
  • Participation in general practice administration and ensuring efficient processes in everyday practice Your profile:
  • Completed training as a medical practice assistant or health specialist, experience in gynecology is an advantage
  • Good IT user knowledge including electronic agenda and medical records
  • Confident use of medical terminology
  • High professional and social skills as well as resilience
  • Team player, motivated, flexible, committed and empathetic personality Our offer:
  • Joining a growing, modern company with future prospects
  • Generous support for your professional development through training and further education opportunities
  • Modern infrastructure, professional work equipment and materials
  • Non-mandatory pension fund solution, we cover 60% of the contributions
  • Workplace with public transport connection (mobility financing), parking garage and SBB half-fare card Interested?: For further information, please contact Ms Fabia Ambros, General Manager Gynecology and Obstetrics, T 041 818 42 01.
