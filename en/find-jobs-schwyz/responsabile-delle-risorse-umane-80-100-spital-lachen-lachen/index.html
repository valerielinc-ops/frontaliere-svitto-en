Full description

Head of Human Resources 80% - 100%

Your tasks

  • Overall responsibility for strategic and operational HR management as well as the personnel and technical management of the HR team with 8 employees
  • Trustworthy sparring partner for the CEO, the management and the executives in all personnel strategy and HR-relevant issues as well as actively shaping and maintaining social partnerships
  • Ensuring needs-based support for the clinics and divisions together with the HR team
  • Strategic development and future-oriented orientation of the HR department with a focus on modern, efficient and digitalized HR processes, systems and structures
  • Development of a holistic, innovative HR strategy and personnel policy as well as consistent transfer of strategic projects into concrete measures and their sustainable implementation
  • Initiation and management of change and development projects as well as active participation in shaping the HR organization with a special focus on the development, optimization and standardization of processes
  • Shaping the management and corporate culture as well as actively positioning Lachen Hospital as an attractive and future-oriented employer
  • Overall responsibility for HR controlling, personnel cost planning and staffing plan management, including definition, analysis and interpretation of relevant HR key figures as well as meaningful reporting for the attention of the management and the board of directors
  • Ensuring and further developing modern, high-quality education and training across all professions

Your profile

  • Several years of proven leadership experience in a higher-level HR management function with overall strategic and operational responsibility; experience in the healthcare sector is an advantage
  • Well-founded training and further education in human resources management (e.g. MAS HRM or Dipl. HR Manager NDS HF)
  • Comprehensive and in-depth knowledge of all relevant HR processes as well as employment and social security law
  • Strong strategic and conceptual skills, combined with analytical thinking, high structuring skills and a consistent implementation orientation
  • Experience in initiating and managing change and digitization projects as well as the ability to put complex projects into practice pragmatically, purposefully and sustainably
  • Enjoy critically questioning the status quo, developing structures and processes, and actively shaping an HR department in a future-oriented manner
  • Engaging, honest and communicative leader with a high level of social competence, who meets people at eye level, creates trust and acts as a convincing designer and source of inspiration

Your benefits

  • Holidays to 49. Age: 25 working days 50. – 59. Age year: 30 working days from 60. Age: 35 working days
  • Seniority gift from 5 years in the form of holidays or wages
  • Extensive training and further education opportunities in the form of working hours and/or cost sharing
  • Employee benefits Possibility of a free room upgrade at Lachen Hospital Permanent discounts on products and services from well-known providers Discounted meals in the staff restaurant Massages and fitness courses at our physiotherapy
  • Accident insurance Worldwide accident cover in the private department at no additional cost
  • Parking Variety of parking spaces at very fair prices
