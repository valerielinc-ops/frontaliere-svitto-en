General information Code 2026-247 DESCRIPTION OF THE AUTHORITY Title Trade Compliance Specialist m/f/d Description of tasks

  • Maintenance, testing and classification of product master data in the area of customs and trade compliance (e.g. customs tariff numbers, country of origin, preference and export control data)
  • Advising and supporting internal stakeholders on customs, export control and trade compliance issues
  • Co-responsibility for implementing and monitoring export control and sanctions requirements
  • Cooperation in cross-departmental customs and compliance projects
  • Monitoring regulatory changes and ensuring regulatory compliance and internal policies
  • Conduct training and awareness-raising activities to promote compliance awareness
  • Support for audits and regulatory audits
  • Preparation and further development of guidelines, work instructions and process documentation in the area of trade compliance
  • Support for the optimization and further development of customs and trade compliance processes in cooperation with international stakeholders
  • Key User for trade compliance applications as well as support for system adjustments and tests Profile
  • Basic technical, logistical or commercial training
  • Further training as a foreign trade specialist with a federal certificate, customs specialist or comparable qualification is advantageous
  • Several years of professional experience in the import, export or customs environment
  • Trade compliance experience and interest in international customs and export control issues
  • In-depth knowledge of the origin of goods, preferential law and supplier’s declarations
  • Knowledge of goods tariffing and master data maintenance (HS codes, CH/EU/UK tariffing)
  • Good user knowledge in MS Office and ideally experience with trade compliance systems
  • Very good knowledge of German and English (level C1) in word and writing
  • Analytical, structured and independent working method
  • High quality orientation, pronounced communication skills as well as pleasure in working with different stakeholders Benefits
  • Unique, family culture with long-term and sustainable corporate management
