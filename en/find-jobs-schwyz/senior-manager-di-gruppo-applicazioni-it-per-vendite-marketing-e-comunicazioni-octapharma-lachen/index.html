Full description

Become part of a vital chain and contribute to our common goal of making people’s lives better. Headquartered in Lachen, Switzerland, Octapharma is one of the largest human protein manufacturers in the world, developing and producing medicines sourced from human plasma and human cell lines. We are a privately-owned company, where the warmth of family meets the scale of a global organisation.

Join us in shaping our vision to provide new health solutions advancing human life.

Your main tasks and responsibilities

  • Partner with Sales, Marketing, and Communications, worldwide, translating business priorities into application roadmaps and digital initiatives.
  • Own the application portfolio lifecycle: demand, business analysis, solution selection, delivery, support, improvement and retirement.
  • Lead demand and service management, including incidents, changes, releases, user support and service performance.
  • Maintain the portfolio roadmap and drive process harmonisation, integration, data quality, reporting and user experience improvements.
  • Lead or oversee CRM, tender management, commercial analytics, digital marketing, communications, website and patient-facing application initiatives.
  • Analyse and shape data, analytics and generative AI use cases, pilots and adoption with Data & AI and specialist teams.
  • Ensure solutions meet architecture, cybersecurity, privacy, data governance, IT compliance, quality and applicable GxP requirements.
  • Coordinate business, IT, global and local teams and suppliers; manage outcomes, plans, risks, dependencies and decisions.
  • Manage supplier performance, contracts, budgets and continuous improvement, and advise leadership on technology opportunities and priorities.

Your expertise and ideal skill set

  • Degree in IT, information systems, business administration or a related field; postgraduate study is an advantage.
  • Significant experience in IT business partnering, enterprise applications, portfolio management or digital transformation.
  • Knowledge of Sales, CRM, Marketing, and Communications applications; life sciences experience is an advantage.
  • Experience leading application roadmaps, demand, solution selection, implementation, service transition and operations.
  • Experience leading global programs and suppliers, managing budgets and contracts, and balancing value, risk, cost and compliance.
  • Working knowledge of architecture, integrations, data and analytics, cloud/SaaS, cybersecurity, privacy and IT service management.
  • Understanding of responsible AI, automation, governance, adoption and value measurement
  • GxP or regulated records experience is an advantage.
  • Strong communication, stakeholder management, analytical and change leadership skills; fluent English and willingness to travel internationally.

Your department - where you make an impact

  • Join Corporate IT Business Applications as senior IT partner to Sales, Marketing, and Communications across the Group.
  • Guide the global application portfolio from business demand and delivery through service, compliance and continuous improvement.
  • Work with business and IT teams to deliver integrated solutions across CRM, tender management, data and AI, websites and digital platforms. The position reports to the Group Senior Director Business Applications.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Schwyz?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Schwyz's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Schwyz's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
