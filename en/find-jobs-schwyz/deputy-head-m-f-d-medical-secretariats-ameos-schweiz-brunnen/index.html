Deputy management (m/f/d) Medical secretariats Shape the future with us in Brunnen! To strengthen our team at the AMEOS Seeklinikum Brunnen we are looking for 1. August 2026 or by arrangement a Deputy Head of the Medical Secretariats. In this versatile and responsible function, you actively support the team in day-to-day operations and ensure the smooth running of administrative processes around our medical services. At the same time, together with the management, you take on professional and organizational management tasks. They participate in the further development of processes, promote efficient cooperation within the team and assume responsibility for the Medical Secretariat in the absence of leadership. In this role, you are an important contact for employees as well as internal and external stakeholders.
