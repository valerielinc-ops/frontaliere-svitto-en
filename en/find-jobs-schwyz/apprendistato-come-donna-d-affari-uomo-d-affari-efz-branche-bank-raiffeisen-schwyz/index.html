02.10.2026 Schwyz Küssnacht Goldau

Become part of our team!

Do you value a solid basic education? Are you a well-established and enthusiastic personality who has good manners, an interest in banking and the pleasure of customer contact? We look forward to your application. For our apprenticeships 2027 we are looking for two communicative, willing and motivated learners (m/f/d) 100%.

What awaits you?

During your three-year training, you will gain insight into different departments and will be used in the following work situations:

  • Receiving and forwarding bank customers
  • Banking clients advise
  • Supporting bank customers via digital channels
  • Assistance in advising customers
  • Administrative preparation and processing of banking transactions

In the various departments, you will be accompanied and supported by competent and experienced practical trainers. You can complete the training with us either as an EFZ (Federal Certificate of Ability) or with a professional degree. Industry education takes place at the CYP in Root and the Vocational School in Schwyz.

This is what we offer you

  • interesting and varied training with a lot of responsibility
  • Flexible work in 6 different departments/locations of 6 months each and further exciting stages
  • 6 Weeks Holiday and Half Tax Subscription Gifted
  • Generous contributions to laptop and language stays
  • Great employee events and more.

What do you bring?

  • Completed secondary level A with good grades
  • very good behavioral grades (social skills)
  • very good knowledge of German (verbal and written)
  • Requirements for learning two foreign languages (French and English)
  • Enjoyment of customer contact and interest in banking
  • Team-ready, reliable and open to new things
  • Flexible, self-responsible and proactive

The following documents are required

  • Letter of motivation
  • CV (incl. photo)
  • all upper-level certificates
  • Result of the multicheck and the signalling test
  • References (class instructor)
  • Test gauge assessments (if any)
