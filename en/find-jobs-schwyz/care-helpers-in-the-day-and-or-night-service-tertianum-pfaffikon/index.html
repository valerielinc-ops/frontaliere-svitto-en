Full description

Your heart beats for the elderly? Is the care profession your calling? Do you see opportunities to meaningfully apply your skills and experiences with us to have a positive impact on the lives of our customers? Then you are perfect for us!

For our Tertianum Residenz Huob facility in Pfäffikon Schwyz, we are looking for a care assistant SRK 50-100% in day and/or night shift immediately or by arrangement.

Your tasks

  • Care and support of assigned guests
  • Assistance with personal care, movement, and other daily activities
  • Offering daily activities for our guests with creative occupations, walks, etc.
  • Taking on tasks as a reference person for daily living according to instructions
  • Participation in care reports and forwarding of important observations
  • Writing care entries in our CareCoach care documentation system (possible with CareCoach-Smartphone)
  • Assistance with logistical and domestic tasks

Your profile

  • Completed SRK care course with internship in long-term care
  • Work experience in long-term care preferable
  • A warm and humorous basic attitude
  • Joy in varied challenges
  • German language skills in speaking and writing

Why Tertianum? We care that our employees can reconcile work and private life well. This includes a diverse range of benefits, for example:

  • Internal and external training opportunities
  • Additional free day in the birthday month
  • Daily lunch for CHF 10
  • Discounted mobile and internet subscriptions with Sunrise
  • Discounts on selected supplementary health insurance
  • SBB half-fare travelcard
  • Various fleet discounts

Further benefits can be found on our website.

Are you interested? We offer you an exciting perspective: With us, you can apply your skills and expertise in a motivated and flexible team. You take on responsibility and help shape the future of Tertianum. Apply now and become part of our team.
