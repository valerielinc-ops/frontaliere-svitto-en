Full description

Your challenge

  • Support in the preparation of quarterly and annual financial statements in financial and operational accounting (e.g. plausibility of the results, creation of reports)
  • Creation and monitoring of master data for operational accounting (e.g. allocations, internal rebookings)
  • Independent processing of inquiries and creation of evaluations with regard to the Ordinance on Integrity and Transparency in the Therapeutic Products Sector (VITH)
  • Support for external statistics and reporting (SpiGes) Your profile:
  • Further training for Specialist in finance and accounting with a federal certificate. Specialist qualification or a business degree with a specialization in finance
  • Several years of experience in financial and management accounting, ideally in the hospital or in a similar industry
  • Proficient in using MS Office, especially an advanced level in Excel
  • Flexibility in terms of time during periods with quarterly and annual financial statements desired
  • Distinctive flair for numbers as well as an analytical, exact, careful and independent way of working
  • Team and service-oriented as well as communicative personality Our offer:
  • Small team with great scope for creativity and a collegial, interdisciplinary working atmosphere
  • Modern infrastructure, professional work equipment and materials
  • Workplace with good public transport connections and a parking garage
  • Joining a growing, modern company with future prospects
  • Non-mandatory pension fund solution, we cover 60% of the contributions
  • Excellent employment conditions, attractive social benefits and many voluntary additional benefits Interested?: For further information, please contact Sandro Schönbächler, Head of Finance and Business accounting, sandro.schoenbaechler@spital-schwyz.ch .
